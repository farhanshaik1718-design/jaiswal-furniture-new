The Bespoke Journey

How Made-to-Order Works

From your initial napkin sketch or floor plan to flawless white-glove installation.

1

Design Consultation

Share your dimensions, preferred wood species, and reference photos. Meet our lead architect in-person or via virtual consult.

2

3D Blueprint & Timber

We generate realistic 3D CAD models and material sample swatches so you inspect grain orientation and ergonomics before cutting.

3

Artisanal Joinery

Seasoned solid hardwood is planed, hand-chiseled with mortise-and-tenon joints, and sealed with 4 coats of organic oils in our workshop.

4

White-Glove Setup

Delivered in custom wooden crates. Our dedicated carpenter crew installs and polishes your piece in place with a 10-year warranty certificate.
