Preserving Your Heirloom Timber

Solid wood is an organic, breathing material that acquires a rich patina over decades. Follow these guidelines to maintain its deep luster:

  • Dust regularly using a soft microfiber cloth along the direction of the wood grain.
  • Wipe liquid spills immediately; do not let standing water rest on polished surfaces.
  • Use heat coasters or placemats under scorching utensils to protect the protective polyurethane coat.
  • Avoid placing furniture directly in front of air conditioning vents or damp exterior walls.
  • Apply natural beeswax conditioner once every 12 to 18 months for perpetual glow.
