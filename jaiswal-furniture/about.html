The Jaiswal Story

Preserving the Soul of Handcrafted Solid Wood.

In an era of fast, flat-pack particle board that ends up in landfills in two years, Jaiswal Furniture was founded on a singular conviction: furniture should be an heirloom passed down through generations.

Our Origins

Born in a Lower Parel Sawmill in 1989

Jaiswal Furniture began with master woodworker Rajesh Jaiswal, who learned the sacred nuances of grain matching and mortise-and-tenon joinery under veteran Rajasthani temple carvers. Starting from a modest 400 sq.ft. carpentry stall in Lower Parel, Mumbai, he refused to ever compromise on solid timber.

Over 35 years, as Mumbai evolved into a metropolis of high-rises and sea-facing penthouses, word of our uncompromising craftsmanship spread among architects, royal families, and connoisseurs. Today, operating from our state-of-the-art 25,000 sq.ft. workshop, we blend German precision sizing with hand-chiseled artistry.

The Principles We Never Break

Our Woodworking Pledge

🌲

Ethical, Seasoned Hardwood

Every log is sustainably harvested from government-regulated plantations. We kiln-dry our timber for 60 to 90 days to achieve optimal 8-10% moisture content, making it perpetually resistant to tropical Indian humidity.

🔨

No Shortcuts or Fillers

Zero MDF, zero particle boards, and zero paper veneers. Drawer backs, underside rails, and internal shelves are all crafted in genuine solid wood. If it carries our emblem, it is solid through and through.

🌿

Healthy, Non-Toxic Living

We hand-rub our pieces with organic hardwax oils, pure beeswax, and low-VOC Italian polyurethane sealants. Your home remains safe from noxious off-gassing and formaldehydes.

Factory Insights

The Jaiswal Workshop Method

Witness how raw logs transform into gallery-worthy centerpieces.

STAGE 01

Grain Bookmatching

Slabs are inspected under raking light to pair harmonious grain swirls and natural wood tones.

STAGE 02

Precision Mortise Joinery

Interlocking wood-on-wood joints that hold firmly under hundreds of kilograms of stress.

STAGE 03

Triple Hand Sanding

Stepped sanding down to 400-grit ensures surfaces feel as smooth as liquid silk.

STAGE 04

Beeswax & Oil Sealing

Coated in organic hardwax oils that protect the timber while preserving tactile warmth.
